<div class="prose">
  <p>Reason alone cannot deliver what is needed once a personal God is granted — not because reason failed, but because what remains to be known depends on a free will that only discloses itself. Revelation was defined as external communication, not private inspiration, and shown to require conditions that separate a genuine claim from an unproven one. Those conditions were applied — first as a live test, then as a filter across actual traditions — narrowing the field to a single remaining candidate, whose positive case has not yet been made.</p>
